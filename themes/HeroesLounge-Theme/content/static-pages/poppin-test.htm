Subs

At least 3 out of 5 people from the core roster must be playing during a match. If a team is unable to field their roster (including substitute players) on time, the ruling “Delay of Map” will apply.

Registering a substitute in the regular season

Substitute players are registered using the HeroicSloth Discord bot before the match starts:

/register-substitute match-url: 11995 sub-url: 13

Full Heroes Lounge addresses work in place of IDs too:

/register-substitute match-url: https://heroeslounge.gg/match/view/11995 sub-url: https://heroeslounge.gg/user/view/13
  • match-url (required) — the match address, or just its ID.
  • sub-url (required) — the substitute's profile address, or just their ID.
  • captain-url (moderators only) — used to register on behalf of another team.

The bot checks the substitute's MMR and division against the match and shows a summary asking you to Confirm or Cancel. Nothing is submitted until you confirm it.

Once you have confirmed, a Discord DM will be sent via the bot to the opposing captain either informing them of an undeniable sub or asking them to approve/deny the sub if one of the criteria is not met. (see "When a substitute can be denied" below for more details)

An unregistered substitute is flagged after the match and turns into a moderation case, which is a far slower conversation than registering up front.

If a last minute sub is needed, a link to the sub’s Heroes Profile page can be sent to the opposing captain in the match lounge instead. In this case, the sub has to be registered as a user on the HeroesLounge website after the match, and then registered as a substitute using the /register-substitute command no later than 24 hours after the match.

Registering a substitute for the playoffs

In the playoffs you register a substitute once for the whole bracket instead of per match:

/register-playoff-substitute playoff-id: 12 sub-url: 7629
  • playoff-id (required) — pick the playoff from the suggestion list.
  • sub-url (required) — the substitute's profile address, or just their ID.

Once registered they stay valid for every match your team plays in that playoff, so there is no need to register them again each round.

Note: a team may register a maximum of five playoff substitutes, so choose them with a little thought rather than registering the first five sloths who answer.

Full playoff rules can be found here.

When a substitute can be denied

A registration may be denied if:

  • The substitute's personal MMR (see MMR and Roster Changes) exceeds the upper bound of the division the match takes place in — Division 1 is open-ended and has no limits.
  • The substitute is part of an active team at least two divisions higher than the division the game takes place in.

The bot checks these automatically. If the substitute is above the MMR limit the registration is not rejected — it's sent to the opposing captain for approval, so register early rather than ten minutes before the match to give them time to respond.

An individual can only be used as a substitute for other teams a maximum of 6 times a season, but a maximum of 3 times for one team. Matches played with a team before an individual leaves still count against that 3-match limit, though not against their overall season total.

Cancelling a registration

To withdraw a registration your team made:

Lists your team's active registrations so you can pick one.
/cancel-substitute
Goes straight to one specific match.
/cancel-substitute match-url: 11995

The opposing captain is notified either way, and once a match has started only a moderator can still cancel a registration.
